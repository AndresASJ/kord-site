Kord's first iPhone release plays your FLAC library. Output depends on the iPhone, audio route, and settings.

## What's landing first

- **FLAC playback** — play lossless files from Apple Files, selected Google Drive files, or your own Jellyfin server.
- **Parametric EQ** — per-band frequency, gain, and Q, with saveable presets.
- **Synced lyrics** — line-by-line highlighting that tracks playback in real time.
- **Smart library** — automatic album/artist grouping with fetched artwork.

## Still in progress

- Library sync across devices
- Gapless playback across album boundaries
- Crossfade and replay-gain options

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. Questions? [Contact support@kordsound.com](mailto:support@kordsound.com).
