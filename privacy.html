Privacy Policy

Your music, your business.

Kord is a local-first music player. We don't collect your data, track your listening, or sell anything to anyone.

Effective date: June 12, 2026

What Kord does

Kord is a native music player for iPhone and Mac. It plays audio files stored on your device or imported from cloud storage you connect. There is no Kord account, no streaming service, and no server that stores your library.

Data we collect

None. Kord does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on any server we operate. Your music library, playback history, EQ settings, and lyrics all stay on your device.

Google Drive access

Kord can optionally connect to your Google Drive so you can import audio files into your local library. When you choose to connect:

  • Kord requests read-only access to your Google Drive files through Google's OAuth flow.
  • Your Google credentials are handled entirely by Google — Kord never sees or stores your Google password.
  • Kord only reads audio files you explicitly select for import. It does not scan, index, or access any other files in your Drive.
  • Imported files are copied to your device's local storage. Kord does not keep a persistent connection to your Drive.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive from Kord at any time in the app's settings, or by revoking access in your Google Account permissions.

Third-party services

Kord may contact the following external services during normal use:

  • MusicBrainz — to look up album and artist metadata (credits, release dates). These are anonymous API requests with no user-identifying information.
  • Last.fm — if you choose to enable scrobbling. This requires you to sign in to your own Last.fm account; Kord sends only the track name, artist, and album for each play.
  • Google Drive — only if you connect it, as described above.

Analytics and tracking

Kord does not include any analytics SDKs, ad trackers, or telemetry. We don't know how many songs you play, which screens you visit, or how often you open the app.

Data stored on your device

Kord stores your library database, playback settings, EQ presets, and cached metadata locally using Apple's standard frameworks (SwiftData, the file system, and the Keychain for OAuth tokens). This data is included in your device backups and is protected by your device passcode and encryption.

Children's privacy

Kord is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because we do not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top and post the new version here. For material changes, we'll note it in the app's changelog.
